    Roadmap to the Platinum — Vice Got a Price!

    Estimates, honestly labelled

    • Difficulty: about 4/10 and about 200 hours to platinum, one playthrough — PowerPyx's published estimates (via EGamers' list roundup).
    • Up to 300 hours or more — Push Square's trophy guide estimate (via Heyday). Both are early estimates, not measured averages; this guide repeats them as estimates only.
    • Online trophies: none. All 55 trophies are offline and single-player (PowerPyx; Push Square via LeonidaUnlocked; KeenGamer).
    • Missables: one flagged, unconfirmed — Positive Mental Attitude (PowerPyx & GTABase). Everything else: not flagged by any source checked.

    Stage 1 — Story first, with two habits (Chapters 1–4 and the named missions)

    Earn the 12 story trophies in order: Just Like Paradise (Gone Fishing), Paradise Lost (Chapter 1), T**s Ass Drug Money (Chapter 2), Vice City, Baby! (Chapter 3), Top Of The World, Ma (Chapter 4), then Trust (Breakfast Run), Break the Bank (The Cartel Heist), Family Matters (Child's Play), DWNFALL (Headliners), Hi-Jacked (VCIA Takedown) and Snow Storm (Operation Savage Glory), saving the final mission for Stage 3. Habit one: meditate at every spot the game offers, starting in Chapter 1 — the first spot is the possibly missable one (see Missables). Habit two: keep a manual save before each chapter ends and before the finale.

    Stage 2 — As you travel: sample, discover, spend

    While the story moves you around Leonida, bank the cumulative counters so they never become an endgame grind: try each of the 9 activity types once (Master of None), discover vehicles toward 100 (Whip It Good) and register 10 in the Scooter Bros app (On the Spot), spend toward $100,000 (Treat Yo Self), launder Marked Bills toward $250,000 (Dime on the Dollar), complete challenges toward 50 (One Last Thing), and make choices whenever offered — PlayStation tracks Actions Speak Louder toward 500. Start every stranger strand you meet; strands unlock in story-gated stages.

    Stage 3 — Finish the story, then the strands

    Complete the final mission for the gold story trophy (You Gotta Know When to Get Off) — from your pre-finale save if you want to revisit endgame choices. Then close out the side content: the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone (five bronzes), Bret's unique-animal hunt (Fair Game), and any strands still open until 8 strands are complete (Strangers & Freaks, silver).

    Stage 4 — Activities & skill trophies

    Clear the activity board: 250 stunts (Cunning Stunts, silver), gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges (The Shootist, silver — easier late, with better stats and weapons), a Top Fun drone-race win at all 14 locations (Top Fun), 20 different race wins (Earn Cash, Not Kudos), beat Cal at fishing (Baited My Tackle), and complete one arcade game (Stick Fiddler). Then the set-pieces: 5 enemies in one Combat Focus (Gunslinger), the unscheduled VCIA departure (Catch Me If You Can), and the bowling follow-through at Kolt & Associates (Let's Go Bowling).

    Stage 5 — Crime board

    Work the robberies as a set, with a getaway planned before each one: 5 bank vaults and escape (Maximum Withdrawal, silver), an armoured truck (A Classic Blitz Play), a jewelry store undetected (Sneakthief), one Illegal Operation from each of the 9 gangs (Pest Control), lose a 4-star wanted level with your partner (Hot Together), and 3 shops back-to-back with your partner without losing the wanted level (Retail Therapy).

    Stage 6 — Collectibles, exploration & the long tail

    Sweep district by district: 3 Collectible Card sets (Don't Touch My Deck, silver), WAiNK at 100 locations (You WAiNKer!, silver), meditate at all 18 spots (Positive Mental Attitude), explore all 52 areas of Leonida (The Goodtime State), buy all 8 garages (Little Boxes), and pick up the one-off scene trophies — the Ocean Beach cocktail (In The Beginning…), the showroom smash (Capital Offense), the melee drive-by (Ten Pin) — plus the deliberately vague hidden ones (Challenging Tank, Personal Best, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There), whose guides in this app say honestly what is and is not confirmed.

    Stage 7 — 100% and the platinum

    Finish with A Long Road: work the in-game 100% checklist category by category until it reads 100%. Published guides agree the checklist opens up fully after the story and that this gold is the longest step of the run. When the last of the 54 pops, Vice Got a Price! — the platinum — unlocks on its own. Congratulations, you're a Leonida Legend.

    Missables & one-time warnings

    ⚠ Flagged as possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude (Bronze)

    What: Meditate at every spot across Leonida — 18 spots (GTABase count).

    Why flagged: PowerPyx and GTABase both flag this as the trophy list's only possibly missable trophy, because the first meditation spot can be used early in the story, in Chapter 1, and it is unconfirmed whether that spot can still be used later. PowerPyx's author meditated there and earned the trophy, so the safe play costs nothing: meditate the moment the game offers it, from the opening chapter on, and keep a manual save before finishing Chapter 1.

    Confidence: flagged by two published guides, confirmed by neither. This app marks it “possible”, not “missable”, on purpose.

    Everything else: not flagged — with three honest cautions

    No other trophy is called missable by any source checked for this guide (PowerPyx, GTABase, KeenGamer, FullSweep, EGamers and CryptoGames roundups of the official list). Story trophies are earned by completing chapters and named missions in order; counts, collectibles, robberies and activities are free-roam work that stays available. “Not flagged” is not a guarantee, so three cheap habits protect the whole run:

    • Rotate manual saves before each chapter ends and before the final mission — the only insurance that costs nothing.
    • Choices accumulate: Actions Speak Louder tracks choices toward 500 across the game; a pre-finale save lets you revisit endgame choices.
    • Vague hidden trophies (Challenging Tank, Personal Best, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There) have deliberately unclear official descriptions and unconfirmed methods — their guides here say so, rather than inventing steps.

    Sources & method

    Every trophy's name, description and grade in this app is the official PlayStation 5 list, exactly as published, carried in app/data/trophies.json. Each trophy's guide is written for this entry in its own words and names its sources beside the guide; a link opens only when you follow it — the app itself fetches nothing and works fully offline.

      How the flags were built

      • Kinds (story, collectibles, activities, side strands, crime, free-roam, completion) are classified from the official descriptions: the 12 story trophies are the chapter, finale and named-mission trophies; counts in descriptions (250 stunts, 42 range challenges, 14 drone locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garages) are quoted as targets.
      • Hidden flags follow the published hidden split by grade — 28 hidden of 55 (gold 1 of 2, bronze 27 of 46, silver and platinum none) — with story trophies hidden; the exact hidden set beyond the story trophies is this guide's best reading of that split, not a Rockstar confirmation.
      • Online: the list has no online trophies — all 55 are offline, single-player — so the Online filter is present and honestly returns none.
      • Estimates (difficulty 4/10, ~200 hours PowerPyx; up to 300+ hours Push Square) are labelled as estimates wherever they appear.
      • No Rockstar artwork: this guide uses no Rockstar Games logo, official artwork, box art or game screenshots. The look is its own.
